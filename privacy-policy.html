1. Introduction

Welcome to DOVA ("we", "our", "us") — a habit tracking application designed to help you build positive routines and eliminate bad habits. Our slogan is "Do What Matters", and that begins with respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy describes how DOVA collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using DOVA, you agree to the practices described here.

💚 Privacy-first promise: DOVA stores your habit data locally on your device. We do not sell your data. Ever.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Habit Data (Local Only)

All your habits, completions, streaks, notes, and personal tracking data are stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable backup.

2.2 Anonymous Analytics

To improve DOVA, we collect anonymous usage statistics via Firebase Analytics. This includes:

  • Feature usage patterns (e.g., which screens are opened)
  • App performance metrics
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version and language settings

This data cannot be linked to you personally. We do not collect your name, email, or habit content.

2.3 Crash Reports

When DOVA crashes, Firebase Crashlytics automatically sends us technical crash logs to help us fix the issue quickly. These reports contain device state and stack traces — never your personal data.

2.4 Google Account (Optional — Backup Only)

If you choose to enable Google Drive Backup, we request access to your Google account solely to:

  • Create a private backup folder in your Google Drive
  • Upload/download your encrypted habit database

We only access files DOVA creates. We cannot see any other files in your Drive.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core habit tracking features
  • To send you local notifications and reminders (all scheduled on-device)
  • To improve app stability and performance
  • To backup and restore your data (only if you enable it)
  • To deliver feature flag updates via ConfigCat

5. Data Storage & Security

Your habit data is stored using industry-standard encryption:

  • Local storage: Encrypted SQLite database on your device
  • Backup files: Stored in your private Google Drive (only if you enable backup)
  • Analytics/Crash data: Anonymized and stored on Firebase servers

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. We recommend enabling device-level protections (PIN, biometric lock).

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We only share data:

  • With the third-party services listed above (as necessary for functionality)
  • If required by law, court order, or governmental regulation
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of DOVA and its users

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data. You can:

  • Access: View all habit data within the app at any time
  • Export: Backup your data to Google Drive whenever you want
  • Delete: Uninstall DOVA to permanently erase all local data
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics via device settings
  • Revoke access: Remove DOVA's Google Drive access from your Google Account permissions

8. Children's Privacy

DOVA is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via the app or update the "Last updated" date above. Please review this policy periodically.
